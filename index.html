ℹ️ What are Wireframes and Why We Use Them?

Wireframes are the architectural blueprints of software design. In the AZE Voice AI platform, interactive wireframes serve as our single source of truth for cross-functional alignment.

🎯 Stakeholder Alignment

Ensures Product Managers, Business Analysts, Designers, and Enterprise Clients share identical expectations on user flow, state management, and edge cases prior to development.

⚡ Rapid Iteration

Permits rapid validation of business rules, quota thresholds (such as the 15-student self-service limit), and LLM cost monitoring flows without incurring database migration overhead.

🧪 End-to-End Testing

Allows QA engineers and AI development agents to test real DOM hierarchies, form validations, modal overlay behaviors, and responsive viewports before backend services go live.
