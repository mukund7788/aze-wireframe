Current Business Problem

  • Manual Work & Delays: Right now, when a new company wants to test the platform, our sales and tech teams have to manually create the account, set up passwords, and assign tests. This takes 1 to 2 days, causing many interested leads to lose interest and leave.
  • Personal Email Spam: When signup is open without restrictions, anyone can sign up using personal emails like Gmail or Yahoo. This creates fake and spam accounts, making it hard to identify real business clients.
  • Empty Account Confusion: When a new client logs into an empty workspace, they cannot see how reports, candidate scores, and speech analysis look in a live setup.
  • Data Pollution: Adding dummy test data directly into a client's real account mixes fake records with real students later on, creating a messy database.

The Proposed Solution

  • Require Business Emails: Only allow sign-ups using official company work emails (block Gmail, Yahoo, etc.). The system will also automatically detect the domain and fill in the company name.
  • Real Client Account with Free Trial: Every new client gets a real, clean account starting at zero. The account comes with a free trial allowance of up to 15 students and a total of 15 free tests per client account (e.g., 1 test per student, or distributed among them up to 15 tests total).
  • Separate Demo Portal Popup: A "Demo Portal" button in the sidebar opens a full-screen popup showing a populated sample account with 34,941 sample candidates, charts, and reports. Clients can explore real-looking data provided by the backend without adding any fake data to their actual account.
  • Quick & Safe Return: Closing the demo popup via the close button (×), "Back to My Account", or the ESC key returns the client directly to their clean real account, ready to invite their real students.
  • Business Value & Sales Growth: Prospective customers can review the platform, understand the flow, and see how the client portal works. Customers can create up to 15 student users and assign tests (up to 15 total per client account) immediately. This will help our AZE system attract new clients and increase sales.

Systems Impacted

  • Client Web Portal (https://azetest.ai/client/login): Signup page with business email validation, clean client dashboard with 15-test trial limit, and the full-screen demo popup.
  • AZE Super Admin Portal (https://azetest.ai/admin/dashboard): Content Management tab for creating demo test templates, and controls for managing trial test and student limits.
  • Backend Assessment Service: Automated account creation, business domain verification, 15-test allocation per account, and student invitation APIs.

Assumptions & Dependencies

  • This feature is for business clients testing the platform.
  • The client account will be treated as a real account, with access limited up to 15 students and 15 total tests.
  • Trial limits (15 students and 15 tests total) can be adjusted by Super Admins if needed.
  • Test templates used for the free trial are created specifically under the Admin Portal's Demo Template section.
  • The demo popup data and the real client account data remain completely separate so real client data is never contaminated.

Business Requirements

Part A: Client Portal (https://azetest.ai/client/login)

1. Authentication & Signup

  • Ability for prospective clients to sign up using only business/work email addresses.
  • Ability for the system to automatically block personal and disposable email providers (such as Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, and Outlook).
  • Ability for the system to automatically detect the email domain and auto-fill the company name.
  • Ability for users to easily switch between the Login and Sign Up screens on the same page.

2. Real Client Account

  • Ability for users to see a clean dashboard showing zero students, zero completed tests, and 15 available trial tests.
  • Ability for users to see how many of their 15 student seats they have used (e.g., 0 of 15 used).
  • Ability for users to invite students by entering their name, email, department, and choosing an assigned test.
  • Ability to give each invited student up to 1 test and a total of 15 tests per client account for free.
  • Ability to see remaining tests in a small box at the bottom of the sidebar.
  • Ability to view helpful empty screens with quick action buttons (such as "+ Invite Student" or "Explore Demo Portal") when no tests or results exist yet.

3. Demo Portal (Sample Showcase Popup)

  • Ability to click "Demo Portal" in the sidebar anytime to open a sample account popup.
  • Ability to view sample charts, 16 active students, and test results in the demo popup.
  • Ability to browse a sample list of 34,941 students to see how search and filters work.
  • Ability to open a complete sample student report (Alexander Wright) to see English scores, pronunciation grades, and voice recordings.
  • Ability to view pricing packages and plans under the Buy Tests tab.
  • Ability to close the demo popup anytime using the "×" button, "Back to My Account", or the ESC key without affecting real account data.
Part B: Super Admin Portal (https://azetest.ai/admin/dashboard)

4. Demo Template Creation & Trial Limits

  • Under Content Management, ability for Super Admins to create Demo Test Templates dedicated specifically for client portal free trials.
  • Ability for Super Admins to assign these demo test templates to the client portal for trial users.
  • Ability for Super Admins to increase or decrease the per-client test allocation (default 15) or per-test user count.
  • Ability for admins to set or change the default free trial limits (15 students and 15 tests each).
  • Ability for admins to see a list of all new trial signups and track their activity.
  • Ability for the system to keep a basic log of who signed up and who invited students for record-keeping.
